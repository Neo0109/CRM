# v1.4.9 Calendar History Picker

Release date: 2026-05-26

## What changed

- Replaced the report history dropdown with a compact calendar picker for Industry Radar and Steam Trends.
- Only dates with saved reports are clickable, so users do not waste time opening empty days.
- Kept the header compact while making longer history ranges easier to scan by month.
- Bumped the visible UI label to `v1.4.9` and the health endpoint version to `v1.4.9-calendar-history-picker`.

## Product note

- History is a time-navigation problem, not a long-list problem. The picker now matches how people remember work: by date and month.
