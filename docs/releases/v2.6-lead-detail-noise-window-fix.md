# v2.6 Lead Detail Noise And Window Hygiene

## Scope

- Product-visible Lead Detail UX.
- Daily sourcing rule hygiene for fresh automation candidates.
- No schema, API, Supabase, login, or manual workflow contract changes.

## Changes

- Reduced Lead Detail noise by consolidating follow-up work into one `跟进动作` area and merging evidence plus Steam link repair into `证据与链接`.
- Kept `Owner`, `Due Date`, and `下一步动作` in one place instead of repeating them across quick-fill and business sections.
- Treated `priority_reason`, `next_action`, and `notes` as human-owned fields: automation defaults them to empty, and known historical automation placeholders display as empty in the detail editor.
- Updated sourcing rules to `sourcing-rules-v6.5-window-hygiene`: candidates launching in fewer than 60 days are dropped or kept as market background, even when they have Demo/试玩 signals.

## Verification

- Frontend contract tests cover the single follow-up action area and placeholder cleanup.
- Lead model tests cover empty default `priority_reason` and no daily-import note injection.
- Daily V4 tests cover near-launch Steam and Bilibili/media candidates routing to drop semantics.
