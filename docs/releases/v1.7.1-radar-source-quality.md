# v1.7.1 Radar Source Quality

- Broadened 行业雷达 from example-driven matching to source-quality and topic-family scoring.

- Clarified in `docs/SOURCING_RULES_V3.md` that user-provided examples are calibration signals, not fixed collection targets.
