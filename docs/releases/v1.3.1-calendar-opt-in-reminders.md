# v1.3.1 Calendar Opt-In Reminders

Date: 2026-05-25

## Changes

- Calendar no longer shows every lead with a `due_date`.
- Lead reminders only appear on the calendar after the user explicitly clicks `加入日历` in the calendar reminder panel.
- Steam official events remain visible by default because they are fixed industry dates.
- Follow-up timing is now chosen from a dropdown: 2 weeks, 1 month, 6 weeks, 2 months, 3 months, 6 months, or a specific date.
- Reminder cards clearly distinguish `已加入日历` from `未加入日历` so the calendar stays intentional and uncluttered.
- Calendar opt-in is stored in hidden lead fields (`calendar_enabled`, `follow_up_interval`) instead of polluting notes.
- Updated health check version to `v1.3.1-calendar-opt-in-reminders`.

## Product Principle

Calendar is treated as a commitment surface, not a dumping ground. Leads should only enter the calendar after a human decides they are worth a future reminder.
