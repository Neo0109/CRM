# Sourcing Rules V2

Date: 2026-05-26

## One-Line Standard

Sourcing is not about finding a few game names every day. It is a system for early discovery, broad funnel screening, pool-based review, and exposure-trail learning.

## Goal

Find projects as early as possible where Bilibili can still enter the China publishing conversation and make the China market materially bigger.

The three keywords are:

- Early
- Accurate
- Actionable

## Daily Report Must Do

Every daily report should separate discovery from BD recommendation.

- Industry insight: explain what changed and why it matters for Bilibili publishing.
- Push pool: only projects worth real BD time now.
- Watch pool: broad funnel candidates that deserve continued tracking.
- Drop pool: projects that should not consume attention again.
- Exposure trail: where the project first appeared, where it spread, and whether we missed the best window.

## Push Pool Standard

Push pool can be empty. It must not be filled just to make the report look productive.

A push-pool lead must satisfy:

- Window is still open, normally at least 60 days before launch.
- Not PC Early Access.
- Not narrative-first or visual-novel-like.
- Not an India-led team.
- No mature publisher with China capability already occupying the role.
- Bilibili can credibly add value through content ecosystem, China community, local communication context, compliance, or market partnership.
- Overseas projects must have either immediately attractive visuals or credible public data.

Near-launch games are market context or drop/watch material, not push recommendations.

## Watch Pool Standard

Watch pool is the main broad funnel.

A watch-pool lead can be less proven, but it still needs a reason to track:

- Fit with Bilibili audience or creator content.
- Visible gameplay, system depth, challenge potential, or strong first-look visuals.
- Some public trail: Steam page, demo, trailer, official site, community, GamesPress, Bilibili, indienova, Discord, QQ, or similar.
- The publishing structure is not obviously locked.

The report should explain why it is worth watching and what signal is missing before BD time is spent.

## Drop Pool Standard

Drop pool should make the team smarter, not just hide bad leads.

Default drop reasons:

- PC Early Access.
- Narrative-heavy or visual novel style.
- India-led team.
- Public reception below 80 percent positive, or clearly weak public signal.
- Formal launch is less than 60 days away and no clear China gap remains.
- Mature publisher already occupies China capability.
- Overseas project has neither high visual appeal nor strong data.

## Lead Fields That Matter Most

For every important lead, prioritize these fields:

- Team
- Genre
- Gameplay
- Progress and launch window
- Publisher or cooperation status
- Team background
- Highlight
- Real contact path
- Official store or website link
- Bilibili fit
- Amplification value
- Exposure trail

## Exposure Trail Standard

For key push and watch leads, record:

- Earliest known source.
- Later spread channels.
- Communities that picked it up.
- Whether the best BD window has been missed.
- Which radar station should track this signal long term.

## Automation Guardrails

The automation should behave conservatively:

- Do not promote near-launch games into push pool.
- Do not force push-pool quantity.
- Keep Steam and SteamDB links for visual/background verification.
- Use notes for concise project insight, not import logs.
- Treat Top Sellers and Popular New as market context unless the China window is still open.
- Use Popular Upcoming, demo/playtest windows, Next Fest, official pages, and community trails for early discovery.
